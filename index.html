TRAIL GUIDE

Pick your piece of the holler

Smoky Hollow has nine neighborhoods, named for places around East Tennessee. Every lot takes on the ground it sits on, so here's how buildable each kind of land is, measured from the town map.

GRASS

Panther Creek

Green banks along the winding creek. The heart of town and the easiest place to build.

Buildable98% of spots
SAND

Cherokee Flats

Wide open plains on the west side. Room to spread out.

Buildable98% of spots
ROCK

Clinch Ridge

Mountainside ledges with a view. Steeper, but about half make a fine cabin spot.

BuildableAbout half
SNOW

English Mountain

Snowy tops on the far east. Surprisingly flat up there.

Buildable87% of spots
